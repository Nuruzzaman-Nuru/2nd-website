My Research Work

Research

Current thesis and research projects

Detection and Classification of Brain Tumor by Using a Deep Learning Framework

Research Supervisors:

Supervisor: Tanpia Tasnim

Lecturer & PC, Dept. of CSE

Co-Supervisor: Fatema Tuj Johora

Lecturer & PC, Dept. of CSE

Research Overview:

A brain tumor is an abnormal growth of cells in the brain, which can be benign or malignant, affecting around 250,000 people globally each year. Early and accurate diagnosis is crucial for effective treatment and improved patient survival. Traditional MRI-based detection is slow and prone to human error. To address this, our research proposes an AI system using YOLOv11 for tumor localization and a custom CNN to classify Glioma, Meningioma, and Pituitary tumors, providing faster and more reliable assistance to doctors.

Key Technologies:
  • YOLOv11 for real-time tumor detection
  • CNN/Custom CNN for precise classification
  • Advanced MRI image processing techniques
Research Goals:

“To enhance the accuracy of medical diagnosis, assist healthcare professionals through automated analysis, and develop real-time tumor detection capabilities.”
